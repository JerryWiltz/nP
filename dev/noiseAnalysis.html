Ideal 3 dB attenuator and 20 dB amplifier

Both components are matched two-ports. The amplifier is unilateral: S11 = S12 = S22 = 0, S21 = 10. Its noise figure is 4 dB. The attenuator and the noise-figure reference are both at 290 K.

This page uses the ideal amplifier constructor. The worked matrices in noiseAnalysis.md use an earlier semi-ideal amplifier with small 0.01 reflections and reverse transmission.
