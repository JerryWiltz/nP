NPN automatic DC bias and RF

2N3904 with a 12 V supply, a 100 kohm / 22 kohm base divider, a 2.2 kohm collector resistor and a 2 kohm emitter resistor bypassed by 10 nF.

All external capacitors: 0.01e-6 F (10 nF). Temperature: 293 K. RF port 1: node 6. RF port 2: node 7. Reference impedance: 50 ohms.

The emitter branch uses sePaRC(2e3, 0.01e-6): a 2 kohm resistor in parallel with a 10 nF bypass capacitor. The resistor sets the DC bias; the capacitor reduces emitter degeneration at RF.
